# Vendor claims

You repeated a vendor's number as a fact, and it was only their claim. A number travels from the company, through someone quoting it, to you, and each step drops a word like "single cluster" or "off-peak". This skill takes the number apart before you repeat it, and writes your line so it says who made the claim.

## The principles
1. A number is a claim until someone outside the vendor checks it. Opening the first source shows they said it, not that it is true.
2. A number is only as big as what was measured. Keep the scope words.
3. A vendor's price is their own price. Say whose price it is.
4. A commentator's math is their estimate, not the vendor's fact.

## Steps
1. Write down the chain: who said it first, where they said it, and who passed it to you. Example: DeepSeek, on its zhihu page, quoted by @swyx on X. Unverified.
2. Copy the exact words around each number, not a summary. Mark what was measured. Example: DeepSeek says 155T tokens a day (per @swyx on X, unverified), and that is a single cluster, not the whole service.
3. For any money number, say whose price it is counted at. Example: DeepSeek says $38 per card-hour (per @swyx on X, unverified), counted at its own off-peak price.
4. Split the vendor's numbers from anyone else's math. Name who did the math and what they assumed. Example: @swyx guesses a GPU costs $5-10 an hour and gets about 80% margin. That is @swyx's estimate, unverified. DeepSeek did not say 80%.
5. Write your line with the claimant's name next to every number and the word "claim" or "unverified" on it. Example: "DeepSeek says one cluster serves 155T tokens a day, per @swyx on X. Unverified."
6. Check the line before it goes out. Does every number have a name next to it? Are the scope and price words still there? Is nothing stated as fact? If any answer is no, fix the line or cut the number.

## Rules
- Never write a vendor number as a bare fact, even in a headline or a chart label.
- Never turn a commentator's estimate into the vendor's claim.
- If you cannot find the first source, say so and keep "unverified".
- If the scope or price words are missing, ask for them or leave the number out.
